Selected projects

Research programs & collaborations

Projects carried out within the Kallam Anji Reddy Molecular Genetics Laboratory, several in collaboration with the Champalimaud Translational Centre for Eye Research (C-TRACER) and partner institutes.

DBT · India

Genomics of familial exudative vitreoretinopathy (FEVR)

Dissecting the role of Norrin/β-catenin pathway genes (NDP, FZD4, TSPAN12, LRP5) in FEVR families from Southern India, identifying novel mutations that segregate with the disease phenotype.

Investigators: Inderjeet Kaur, Ganeswar Rao Musada, Subhadra Jalali, Subhabrata Chakrabarti and colleagues.

DBT · India

Biomarkers for risk prediction in retinopathy of prematurity (ROP)

An integrated molecular genetics, transcriptomics and proteomics program to identify predictive markers of severe ROP in preterm infants and inform anti-angiogenic and early-intervention strategies. Vitreous and plasma proteomics undertaken with CCMB, Hyderabad.

Investigators: Inderjeet Kaur, Sonika Rathi, Subhadra Jalali, Ramesh Kekunnaya, Subhabrata Chakrabarti and colleagues.

NIBMG · India

Genomic prediction of high-risk tumors in uveal melanoma (UM)

The first whole-exome study of uveal melanoma in an Indian patient, identifying somatic mutations in GNAQ and SF3B1 and additional candidate genes relevant to tumour development, metastatic evolution and management.

Investigators: Inderjeet Kaur, Santosh Honavar, Mohammad Javed Ali, Subhabrata Chakrabarti, Partha P. Majumder and colleagues.

DBT · India

Functional genomics of primary open-angle glaucoma (POAG)

Genome-wide expression analysis of a POAG cohort, screening candidate genes in the complement and extracellular-matrix (ECM) pathways and correlating ECM-associated genotypes with aqueous-humour protein levels and treatment response.

Investigators: Subhabrata Chakrabarti, Inderjeet Kaur, Meha Kabra, Kollu N. Rao, Rohit C. Khanna and colleagues.

Indo-Australia Biotech Fund · DBT

Runs of homozygosity for new genes in primary congenital glaucoma (PCG)

A microarray-based homozygosity-mapping approach (~1.8 million variants) to discover novel PCG genes in cases without CYP1B1 or LTBP2 mutations, identifying candidate loci on chromosomes 19 and 20 for resequencing.

Investigators: Subhabrata Chakrabarti, Sriparna Ganguly, Inderjeet Kaur, Anil K. Mandal, Sirisha Senthil, Partha P. Majumder.

Univ. of Heidelberg · Germany

Central India Eye and Medical Diseases Study (CIEMS)

A clinical and population-genomic study of 5,000 rural individuals over 40 in the Nagpur region, dissecting the molecular basis of quantitative ocular and systemic traits, including risk factors for early AMD, using next-generation sequencing.

Investigators: Subhabrata Chakrabarti, Vinay Nangia, Kollu N. Rao, Meha Kabra, Inderjeet Kaur and colleagues.

Vision CRC · Australia

Genetic susceptibility to contact-lens-related adverse events

Analysis of immune-regulatory genes (interleukins, toll-like receptors, defensins) by customised genotyping microarray to determine genetic associations with contact-lens-related adverse events in an Indian cohort.

Investigators: Subhabrata Chakrabarti, Nagaraju Konda, Nerida Cole, Inderjeet Kaur, Mark Willcox.

DST · Indo-Tunisia program

Genetics of primary congenital glaucoma: India, Brazil & Tunisia

Comparative genotype–phenotype and founder-effect analyses of CYP1B1 mutations in PCG across Indian, Brazilian and Tunisian cohorts, tracing the origin, migration and diversification of prevalent mutations such as G61E and 4340delG.

Investigators: Subhabrata Chakrabarti, Anil K. Mandal, Inderjeet Kaur and international collaborators.
