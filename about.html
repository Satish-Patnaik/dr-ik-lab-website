Biography

Dr. Inderjeet Kaur

Dr. Inderjeet Kaur is a Scientist in Molecular Genetics at the L V Prasad Eye Institute (LVPEI), Hyderabad, working within the Kallam Anji Reddy Molecular Genetics Laboratory at the Prof. Brien Holden Eye Research Centre.

She obtained her Master's in Forensic Science from Punjabi University, before completing doctoral studies in molecular human genetics at Guru Nanak Dev University (GNDU), Amritsar. She served as a lecturer at GNDU before joining LVPEI to pursue research on the molecular genetics of eye disease.

Her central interest is understanding the molecular mechanisms underlying complex eye diseases, namely age-related macular degeneration (AMD), myopia, and glaucoma. More recently, her laboratory has focused on identifying biomarkers for the retinopathies (diabetic retinopathy, retinopathy of prematurity, and AMD) and on the role of neuron-glia interactions in the neurovascular conditions of the eye.

Her work has been published in peer-reviewed international journals and supported by extramural grants from government agencies including the Department of Biotechnology (DBT) and Department of Science and Technology (DST). Her research on AMD and myopia has been widely recognised as pioneering work in the field from India.
