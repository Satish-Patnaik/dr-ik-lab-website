The laboratory

Our team

The scientists, scholars and research staff driving our work on the genetics and biology of vision-threatening eye disease.

Faculty

Principal investigators

Dr. Inderjeet Kaur
Principal Investigator · Scientist
Molecular Genetics

Dr. Kaur completed her doctoral studies in molecular human genetics at Guru Nanak Dev University (GNDU), Amritsar, and is a Scientist at LVPEI, where she leads research on the genetics, biomarkers and biology of complex eye diseases.

Postdoctoral researchers

Research scientists

Dr. Durairaj Duraikannu
Project Research Scientist-I
CAR-ICMR

Durairaj received his PhD from the Vision Research Foundation (affiliated to Anna University), Chennai, and gained postdoctoral experience on myopia and glaucoma in Dr. Nair's lab, Department of Ophthalmology, UCSF, San Francisco, USA. At LVPEI he focuses on developing retinal glial cells from human induced pluripotent stem cells and exploring the role of neurodegeneration in retinopathies.

Dr. Pallavi Goel
Project Research Scientist-I
ICMR Intermediate

Pallavi received her PhD in Animal Biotechnology from ICAR–National Dairy Research Institute (NDRI), Karnal. Her research focuses on diabetic retinopathy and glaucoma, investigating EV-mediated intercellular communication, inflammation, oxidative stress, fibrosis and neurodegenerative mechanisms through integrated molecular and functional approaches.

Sarmeela Sharma
Project Research Scientist-I
CAR-ICMR

Sarmeela obtained her master's in biotechnology from Shri Mata Vaishno Devi University and is a Senior Research Fellow at LVPEI. Her project focuses on understanding the role of autophagy in the pathogenesis of diabetic retinopathy.

Doctoral researchers

PhD scholars & research fellows

Samir Bera
Senior Research Fellow
CAR-ICMR Technical Support-III

Samir completed his master's in animal biotechnology from the University of Hyderabad and is pursuing doctoral studies under the guidance of Dr. Subhabrata Chakrabarti, focusing on the genetics of primary congenital glaucoma.

Neha Sharma
Senior Research Fellow
CAR-ICMR Technical Support-III · DST INSPIRE

Neha completed her master's from HNB Garhwal University, Uttarakhand, and is a PhD scholar funded by the DST INSPIRE fellowship. Her research focuses on the genetic predisposition, immune activation and inflammation contributing to the pathophysiology of retinopathy of prematurity.

Rodoshie Ghosh
Junior Research Fellow
Lady Tata Memorial Trust

Rodoshie completed her M.Tech in Biotechnology from KIIT University and is a Junior Research Fellow at LVPEI. Her PhD research focuses on predicting the risk of retinopathy of prematurity (ROP) in small-for-gestational-age infants born preterm, using metabolic and DNA-methylation profiles to identify biomarkers associated with ROP development.

Kaulick Mitra
Junior Research Fellow
CSIR-NET

Kaulick completed his master's in biotechnology from BRIC-NIBMG with a specialisation in biomedical genomics. He is pursuing doctoral studies under the guidance of Dr. Inderjeet Kaur and is interested in exploring the role of neurodegeneration in retinopathies.
